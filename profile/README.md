## ⚙️ What ERPNext Brings to Business Management Workflows

ERPNext is an enterprise resource planning platform designed to connect business functions through a shared system. Its modular structure helps organisations coordinate records and activities across departments instead of relying on disconnected applications.

The accounting module supports financial workflows involving sales invoices, purchase invoices, expenses, journal entries, and payment records. Businesses can organise transaction information and review financial activity through reports configured for their operations.

Bank reconciliation tools help compare recorded transactions with bank statements. This process supports the review of payment entries, identification of discrepancies, and maintenance of consistent financial records.

Customer relationship management features provide a structured place for leads, opportunities, customer details, and sales communications. Teams can use these records to coordinate follow-ups and maintain visibility into customer interactions.

Sales workflows connect quotations, orders, invoices, and delivery-related records. Linking these activities helps staff follow transactions as they progress from an initial customer request toward fulfilment and payment.

Purchasing capabilities help manage supplier information, requests, purchase orders, and incoming goods. Connecting purchasing records with inventory and accounting can improve visibility across procurement activities.

Inventory management supports item catalogues, warehouse records, stock transfers, receipts, and delivery transactions. Barcode-related workflows can assist with identifying products and processing inventory activities where the required configuration and equipment are available.

ERPNext also includes project management functions for task assignment, milestones, time tracking, and project-related records. These tools can help teams organise responsibilities and monitor work within a shared operational environment.

The platform supports multiple companies and currencies when configured appropriately. These capabilities can be useful for organisations that manage separate business entities or conduct transactions involving different currencies.

Customisation options allow administrators and developers to adapt forms, fields, workflows, permissions, and reports to organisational requirements. The Frappe framework provides a foundation for extending applications and integrating additional business logic.

Integration possibilities can connect ERPNext with external services through supported APIs, webhooks, and compatible applications. The suitability of a particular integration depends on the service, available connector, configuration, and maintenance requirements.

Deployment arrangements vary according to infrastructure and operational needs. Organisations can evaluate managed hosting, cloud infrastructure, or self-managed environments while considering administration, backups, access controls, updates, and ongoing maintenance.

ERPNext is an open-source project maintained within the Frappe ecosystem. Its source code, documentation, and community resources can help administrators and developers understand its architecture, configure workflows, and investigate implementation questions.

## 🚀 Starting an ERPNext Session

**Prerequisites:** A reachable ERPNext instance, a compatible web browser, and an account with the permissions required for your intended tasks. Server deployment may require additional infrastructure and administration.

1. **Prepare the Environment:** Decide whether to use an existing hosted instance or arrange a deployment that meets your organisation's technical requirements.
2. **Open ERPNext:** Select the GET — ERPNext button above to continue to the designated project access page, then follow the available instructions.
3. **Connect to Your Instance:** Open the ERPNext address provided by your administrator or hosting setup.
4. **Sign In:** Enter your authorised account credentials and confirm that your account has access to the necessary modules.
5. **Configure Business Records:** Review company settings, user permissions, currencies, chart of accounts, warehouses, and other applicable configuration before processing live transactions.
6. **Verify the Workflow:** Test the relevant process with appropriate sample or approved records, check reports and permissions, and confirm that backups and operational procedures are in place.
